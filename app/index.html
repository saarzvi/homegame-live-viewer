Help & support

Here to help.

Home Game runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later. English is the default on new installs; Hebrew and Arabic are available in the app's language settings.

Beta testers can contact the developer privately using TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback option. Please do not include personal player information, financial details or private game records in feedback.

TestFlight helpPrivacy information
How do I get started?

Open Groups, create a group, add players and choose the group. Open Tonight to record a session.

Does Home Game provide gambling or payments?

No. Home Game is a record-keeping utility for activities conducted outside the app. It does not offer gameplay, wagers, money transfers or prizes.

Where are my records stored?

Game records are stored locally on your device. Operating-system backups may retain copies. The current app does not send game records to a developer-operated server.

Can I share my records?

Yes. You choose when and with whom to share a summary using the system share sheet. Copies shared with others are outside the app's control.

How can I delete a group or its history?

Use the group deletion controls in Groups. Deleting a group removes its session history; players associated with other groups may remain. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to backup and restoration settings.

How do I change the language?

Open Groups, tap the globe icon and select English, Hebrew or Arabic. Existing names and records are not automatically translated.
